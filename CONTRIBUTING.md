# Contributing

Contributions are welcome only when their origin and permission are clear.

By submitting a change, you confirm that:

* you are authorized to contribute it under the MIT License;
* it contains no Microsoft, employer, customer or other third-party confidential
  information;
* examples and tests use fictional, non-identifiable data;
* copied or generated material is identified with its source and applicable
  license or permission; and
* it does not create a support, maintenance, response-time, roadmap, warranty,
  certification or availability commitment.

Do not submit customer-specific features, private implementation details,
credentials, tenant identifiers, production data or requests tied to purchasing,
account, support or other commercial decisions.

Security reports belong in the private channels listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md),
not in a public issue.
