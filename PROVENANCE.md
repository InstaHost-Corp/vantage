# Provenance boundaries

This public record documents the project's non-sensitive source boundaries. A
more detailed creation and resource-separation record is retained privately for
outside-work and ownership review.

* Git history identifies the contributors to repository-authored source.
* `web/package-lock.json` records frontend packages and integrity hashes.
* React, React Router, Tailwind CSS and Vite are MIT licensed; Lucide is ISC
  licensed.
* `web/dist` is generated from `web/src` and is checked for build parity by
  `node scripts/verify-invariants.mjs`.
* Seeded organizations, people, accounts, systems and evidence are fictional.
* Framework requirement labels are short paraphrased summaries for an
  educational demonstration. They are not copies of standards and do not
  provide authoritative implementation or legal guidance.
* No Microsoft or customer confidential information, code, prompts, designs,
  schemas, logs, screenshots, data or roadmap material may be contributed.

Unknown or disputed origins must be preserved and reviewed rather than rewritten
or deleted to manufacture provenance.
